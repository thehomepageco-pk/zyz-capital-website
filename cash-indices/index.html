How cash indices work

A cash index CFD tracks the spot value of an underlying stock index. Unlike futures contracts, cash indices have no expiry — you hold the position for as long as your strategy requires, subject to daily swap charges (waived entirely on Islamic accounts).

Indices are ideal for expressing a macro view: trade the US technology cycle through NAS100, European industrial strength through DAX40, or Asian sentiment through JP225 and HK50.
