FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I hold more than one account type?+

You can manage available trading accounts through the Client Portal. Contact the support desk if you need help choosing or adding an account.

Which account is best for a beginner?+

The Standard account has the lowest minimum deposit ($10) and spread-based pricing. Consider a demo first and choose based on your experience and risk tolerance.

What is the difference between Pro and Bullion?+

Both offer 0.0 pip spreads and premium execution across bank, brokerage and client liquidity. Pro starts from $5,000, while Bullion starts from $20,000 and carries the highest tier of execution priority and service.

Do you offer Islamic swap-free accounts?+

Yes. A swap-free option is available on request and never charges or pays overnight interest, making it suitable for Sharia-compliant traders.
