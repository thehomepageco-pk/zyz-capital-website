What is the best time to trade forex in Sri Lanka?+

Liquidity is usually deepest when the London and New York sessions overlap, which is between 18:30 and 22:30 Sri Lanka Standard Time (GMT+5:30) in Colombo. Times move one hour earlier while Europe and the US are on summer time.
